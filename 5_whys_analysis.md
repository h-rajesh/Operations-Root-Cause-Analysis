# 5 Whys — Backlog Increase

**Problem:** Simulated backlog increased.

1. Why did backlog increase? Processing output was below the illustrative target.
2. Why was output below target? One work area may have been slower.
3. Why may that area have been slower? Work distribution may not have matched demand.
4. Why might distribution not have matched demand? Allocation may not have been reviewed during the shift.
5. Why might review have been missed? A standardized workload review routine may not have been defined.

**Validation required:** Review hourly demand, staffing, equipment downtime, process times and input availability before accepting any root cause.
