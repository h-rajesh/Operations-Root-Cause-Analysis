# Operational Root Cause Analysis & Bottleneck Reduction

## Problem statement
In this simulated operation, processing delays and backlog are elevated during the baseline period. This analysis structures hypotheses and proposes actions; it does not claim that the causes have been verified.

## 4M analysis
### Man (People)
- Staffing distribution
- Training gaps
- Work allocation
- Attendance assumptions

### Machine (Equipment)
- Equipment downtime
- Maintenance assumptions
- Equipment capacity
- Workstation availability

### Method (Process)
- Inefficient workflow
- Unclear task sequence
- Inconsistent standard procedures
- Handover problems

### Material (Inputs)
- Material availability
- Supply placement
- Package flow
- Label or documentation issues

## 5 Whys — hypothesis example
Problem: backlog increased.
1. Why? Processing output was below the simulated target.
2. Why? One workflow section may have operated more slowly.
3. Why? Work distribution may not have matched demand.
4. Why? The shift allocation plan may not have been updated.
5. Why? A standardized workload review may have been missing.

These are hypotheses to test, not verified conclusions.

## Measurement plan
Compare baseline and follow-up periods using:
- Backlog
- Packages processed
- Delay minutes
- Defect rate
- Staffing levels

Do not attribute any change to a corrective action unless the intervention, timing and other relevant conditions are documented. The included before/after data is synthetic and intentionally illustrative.
